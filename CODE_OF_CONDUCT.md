# Code of conduct

Everyone participating in PixelForge Prompt Studio's issues, pull requests
and other official project spaces is expected to treat others with dignity.
People of all backgrounds and experience levels are welcome.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient and clear. Critique an idea or patch, not a person.
- Respect different viewpoints and communicate disagreement constructively.
- Welcome questions, acknowledge mistakes and give actionable feedback.
- Respect privacy, consent, authorship and other people's time.

Harassment, threats, hateful or discriminatory language, sexual harassment,
doxxing, impersonation, spam and repeated disruptive behavior are not allowed.
Do not publish someone else's private information or private correspondence.
The policy also covers representing the project in outside public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Use GitHub's content-reporting tools for abusive content. For a concern that
needs maintainer coordination, request a private reporting channel through a
minimal issue without names, evidence or sensitive details; wait for a
confirmed private channel before sharing them. Do not use public threads to
investigate allegations. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
For platform abuse or urgent threats, use GitHub's reporting and safety
channels rather than waiting for volunteer maintainers.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, warn participants, restrict
interaction or exclude repeat/severe offenders. Decisions consider severity,
impact and prior behavior. Reports should be handled discreetly and in good
faith; absolute confidentiality or a response deadline cannot be guaranteed.
Retaliation against someone reporting in good faith is itself a violation.

You may ask the maintainer to reconsider a decision, using the same discreet
coordination process. State the concern and any relevant new information;
do not repeatedly relitigate a closed decision through unrelated threads.

This is an original project policy, not a claim of external certification.
